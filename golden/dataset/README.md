# Frozen dataset of the golden routes

One service day, Tuesday 2026-10-27, built by `scripts/freeze-dataset.sh` from the feeds of 2026-10-26 and Geofabrik's OpenStreetMap of 2026-10-27: every TBM trip of that day, the SNCF trips that stop in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, and the streets of OpenStreetMap around the golden routes, in Bordeaux and Thenon. `bikes_allowed` is left as published: the pipeline applies the bike rules in CI.
